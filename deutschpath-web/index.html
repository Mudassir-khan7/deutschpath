Daily Reminder
1
Anki Every Single Day

Even 10 minutes. Spaced repetition is what makes vocabulary stick permanently.

2
Always Learn Nouns With Gender

Always learn der/die/das with every word. Never memorize a bare noun alone.

3
Speak Out Loud Daily

Silent learning doesn't build real fluency. Talk out loud even when studying by yourself.

4
Sundays are for Review

Don't skip review days. Consolidation converts short-term knowledge into long-term recall.
